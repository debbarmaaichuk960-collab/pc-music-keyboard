KeyPilot Privacy Policy

Your Keyboard, Your Control.

Effective date: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KeyPilot handles information when you use the KeyPilot Android keyboard application.

1. Information and keyboard input

KeyPilot processes text locally to provide keyboard input and suggestions. KeyPilot does not operate a server to collect or store the text you type. Your text is delivered to the app or field where you are typing through Android's keyboard system.

2. Learned words and personalization

When you choose word suggestions, accepted words may be saved in the app's private storage on your device to improve future suggestions. Keyboard preferences and custom theme images are also stored locally. You can clear learned words in KeyPilot Settings and delete saved theme images from the My themes section.

Suggestions and word learning are disabled in password and other sensitive or no-suggestions fields.

3. Microphone and voice typing

Voice typing is optional. It starts only when you tap the microphone and grant microphone permission. KeyPilot uses the Android speech recognition service available on your device. Depending on that service and your device settings, speech may be processed on-device or sent to the service provider online. The provider's own privacy terms apply to its processing.

KeyPilot does not create or store audio recording files. Voice typing is unavailable in password fields. You can turn voice typing off in KeyPilot Settings or revoke microphone permission in Android settings.

4. Sharing text

If you explicitly choose the Share feature in the keyboard, KeyPilot passes the selected text, or the available text around the cursor when no selection is present, to the sharing app you choose. The receiving app handles that information under its own privacy policy. KeyPilot does not share text automatically.

5. Permissions

  • Microphone: used only for optional voice typing after you grant permission.
  • Vibration: used for optional key-press feedback.

6. Data storage and security

Keyboard preferences, saved theme images and learned words are kept in the app's private storage on your device. This version of KeyPilot does not provide a KeyPilot account or cloud synchronization service. No method of electronic storage is completely risk-free, so keep your device protected.

7. Your choices

You can turn off voice typing, revoke microphone permission, clear learned words, remove saved theme images, or uninstall KeyPilot. Uninstalling the app removes its private app data according to Android's standard app-removal behavior.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The effective date at the top will be revised when a new version is published.
